## Main findings reflected in the submitted paper

- 320 model executions with a paired experimental design and common Lloyd refinement.
- All 320 frozen prototype banks evaluated on 10,000 held-out official CIFAR-10 test images.
- SOM-minus-winner test quantization reductions of 1.3511% (1k RGB), 0.7954% (1k PCA-64), and 0.4174% (5k RGB), significant after the twelve-comparison Holm correction.
- Test ARI lower in the two 1,000-image conditions (Holm-adjusted p = 0.029844 and 0.020044).
- No test NMI contrast significant after Holm correction.
